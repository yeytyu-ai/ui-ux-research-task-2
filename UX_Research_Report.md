# UX Research and Problem Definition

## 1. Project Title
Student Study Planner App

## 2. Introduction
The Student Study Planner is a mobile application designed to help students organize their academic activities, assignments, exams, and daily study schedules.

## 3. Problem Statement
Many students find it difficult to manage their time, remember assignment deadlines, and prepare for examinations. A simple study planner can help students organize their tasks and track their progress.

## 4. Target Users
- College students
- School students
- Students preparing for competitive examinations

## 5. User Personas

### Persona 1: Busy College Student
Name: Priya
Age: 20
Goal: Manage classes, assignments, and examinations.
Pain Points: Forgets deadlines and struggles to plan study time.

### Persona 2: Competitive Exam Student
Name: Rahul
Age: 21
Goal: Follow a consistent preparation schedule.
Pain Points: Finds it difficult to track subjects and daily targets.

### Persona 3: School Student
Name: Anu
Age: 16
Goal: Complete homework and revise lessons on time.
Pain Points: Needs reminders and a simple task list.

## 6. Competitive Analysis

### Google Calendar
Strengths: Scheduling, reminders, and calendar views.
Limitations: Academic planning requires manual organization.

### Todoist
Strengths: Task lists, priorities, and due dates.
Limitations: Students may need additional tools for academic schedules.

### MyStudyLife
Strengths: Designed for managing classes, exams, and assignments.
Limitations: Users need to enter and maintain their schedules.

## 7. User Pain Points
- Forgetting assignment deadlines
- Difficulty creating daily study plans
- Poor time management
- Lack of progress tracking
- Difficulty organizing multiple subjects

## 8. Proposed Solutions
- Easy-to-use daily and weekly study planner
- Assignment and exam reminders
- Subject-wise task organization
- Progress tracking
- Simple and clear interface

## 9. Conclusion
UX research helps identify student needs and problems. The proposed Student Study Planner App aims to make academic planning easier through reminders, organized schedules, and progress tracking.
